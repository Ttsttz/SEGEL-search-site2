SEGEL

About this database

SEGEL (Smoking Effects on Gene Expression of Lung) contains eight public expression microarray data sets from trachea epithelial cells, large airway epithelial cells, small airway epithelial cells, and alveolar macrophage. The microarray data sets visualizes the effects of smoking on gene expression in different human cells in the lungs. The database supports genes associated with multiple probe IDs, allowing users to retrieve all available documents for a given gene.

How to use: Enter a gene symbol (e.g., PAX8, DDR1, or TP53) to retrieve the corresponding microarrays.
